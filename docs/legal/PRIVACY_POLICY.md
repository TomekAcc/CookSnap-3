## 2. The short version

- There are **no accounts**. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
- Your **saved recipes, pantry, scan history and settings stay on your phone.**
- To read a photo or write recipes, the app sends the **photo or the ingredient list to our server**,
  which passes it on to **Google's Gemini AI** and returns the answer. We do not store the photo or the
  answer on our server.
- We use **no advertising, no tracking and no analytics** at the time of writing.

## 3. What we process, and why

| Data | Where it goes | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Photos of your fridge or pantry** (resized by the app to about 1280 px) | Your phone, then our server, then Google's Gemini API | To detect the ingredients in the photo | Performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Not stored by us. | **Ingredient lists, meal type, servings, hunger level, language** | Same route | To write recipes and meal plans | Art. 6(1)(b) | Not stored by us |

## 4. Photos and the AI

Photos and prompts are processed by **Google LLC** through the paid Gemini API as our service provider.
Google receives the photo or text but not your name or install ID. According to Google's terms for its
paid Gemini API, Google does not use this content to train its models; it may keep it for a limited
period for abuse monitoring ([VERIFY the current wording of Google's terms before publishing]). See
Google's Gemini API terms and privacy information for details.

The AI can make mistakes. Please do not photograph people or documents. Photos should show food,
shelves and containers.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to, correction, deletion or restriction of your data, object
to processing based on our legitimate interest, withdraw consent given earlier, and receive your data in
a portable form.

Because we keep almost no data about you (the only record on our side is a random install ID counter
that disappears the next day, and we cannot link it to you), in most cases there is nothing to hand over
or delete beyond what is on your own phone, which you control: delete saved items inside the app or
delete the app. Write to **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** if you want to exercise any right. You also have the right
to complain to the Polish data protection authority, the **President of the Personal Data Protection
Office (UODO)**, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl, or to the authority in your own country.

## 8. Children

CookSnap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you
believe a child has used the app, contact us.

## 9. Security

Requests between the app, our server and Google are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our server holds the
Google key as a protected secret, accepts only the request types the app sends, and limits the number of
requests per phone per day. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the
app if a change is material.
