## 2. The short version

- There are **no accounts**. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the app.
- Your **saved recipes, pantry, scan history and settings stay on your phone.**
- To read a photo or write recipes, the app sends the **photo or the ingredient list to our server**,
  which passes it on to **Google's Gemini AI** and returns the answer. We do not store the photo or the
  answer on our server.
- We use **no advertising, no tracking and no analytics** at the time of writing.
- Google's AI **does not learn from your photos** under the terms we use (see section 4), and you can **delete everything the app keeps on your phone** in one tap: Settings > About > "Delete all my data".

## 4. Photos and the AI

Photos and prompts are processed by **Google LLC** through the paid Gemini API as our service provider.
Google receives the photo or text but not your name or install ID. According to Google's terms for its
paid Gemini API, Google does not use this content to train its models; it may keep it for a limited
period for abuse monitoring ([VERIFY the current wording of Google's terms before publishing]). See
Google's Gemini API terms and privacy information for details.

**This is AI-generated content.** Ingredient lists and recipes are produced by an AI model and can be wrong,
including about allergens. The app tells you so, and our Terms of Service explain what you must check
yourself. **We do not use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects**,
and there is no profiling: the AI only turns your photo and choices into an ingredient list and recipes.

Please do not photograph people, children or documents. Photos should show food, shelves and containers.

## 5. Who else is involved

- **Google** (Gemini API): reads photos and writes recipes.
- **Cloudflare, Inc.**: runs the server that sits between the app and Google.
- **Expo** (EAS Update): delivers app updates.
- **Telegram** (optional): receives only a short notice that a new message has arrived, never the message, your reply address or anything you wrote. It is a notification channel for us, not a recipient of your data.
- **Apple / Google Play**: distribute the app and process payments.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.**: confirms whether a subscription is active (anonymous ID and purchase status only).

Google, Cloudflare and (when subscriptions are available) RevenueCat act as our service providers (processors) under their
data-processing terms. Apple, Google Play and Expo run their own platforms. We do not sell your data and we do
not share it for advertising or tracking.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion ("to be forgotten") or restriction,
object to processing based on our legitimate interest, withdraw consent given earlier (this does not affect
what was done before), and receive your data in a portable form. You are not required to give us any personal
data to use the app; the only thing that works less well without sending photos to the AI is scanning.

**What you can do yourself, right now, in the app:**

- **Delete everything on your phone:** Settings > About > "Delete all my data" removes saved recipes, pantry,
  scan history, preferences, the unsent-feedback outbox, the install ID and the stored scan photos.
- **Stop sending photos to the AI:** Settings > About > "Send photos to AI" withdraws your consent. The app asks
  again before the next scan.
- **Remove a single scan** from Scan History, or switch a diet preference off at any time.
- **Backups:** your phone's own backup (iCloud, Google) may keep a copy of app data. Remove it in your phone's
  backup settings if you want it gone there too.

**What you can ask us to do:** write to **cooksnap.support@gmail.com** to exercise any right. We answer within
one month. The only data about you on our side is (a) the random install ID counter, which disappears the
next day and which we cannot link to you, and (b) any Help & Feedback message you sent, which we delete
after 90 days. If you want a message deleted sooner, send us the text or the approximate time and we will
delete it. We may need a little information to find it, because we do not know who you are.

You also have the right to complain to the Polish data protection authority, the **President of the Personal
Data Protection Office (UODO)**, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl, or to the authority in your own
country.

## 8. Children

CookSnap is meant for people aged **16 or over** and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect
data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help remove what we hold.

## 9. Security

Requests between the app, our server and Google are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our server holds the Google
key as a protected secret, accepts only the request types the app sends, and limits the number of requests per
phone per day. The private page where we read feedback is protected by a long secret. No method of transmission
or storage is perfectly secure.

If a security incident puts your personal data at risk, we will notify the supervisory authority within 72
hours where the law requires it, and tell you without undue delay if the risk to you is high.

## 10. What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps your data in its own storage on the phone and a random install ID. This is needed for the app to
work and to apply the daily request limit, so under EU rules on storing information on a device it does not
need a separate consent. There are no cookies, no advertising ID, no fingerprinting and no tracking.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the
app if a change is material.
