# CookSnap — Health and Food Disclaimer

**Last updated:** 9 November 2026

CookSnap is a cooking-inspiration tool. It is **not** a dietitian, a doctor, an allergy test or a food-safety authority.
This page explains what that means for you. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## 1. AI can be wrong

Ingredient lists and recipes are produced by an artificial-intelligence model. They can be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for you.
The app can miss an item, name it wrongly, invent one that is not in your photo, or suggest quantities, times,
temperatures or methods that are not right. Check every result before you cook.

## 2. Allergies and intolerances

The diet switches (for example nut-free, lactose-free, gluten-free, vegan) and the "Always avoid" list are a convenience,
**not a guarantee**. The app cannot see allergens hidden in packaged food, sauces, spices or "may contain" warnings, and it
cannot know about cross-contact in your kitchen (shared boards, pans, oil, utensils).

**If you or anyone you cook for has an allergy, coeliac disease or another intolerance, always read the labels and check each
ingredient yourself. Do not rely on the app.** Severe allergies can be life-threatening.

## 3. Nutrition figures

Calories, macronutrients and similar values are **estimates** made by the AI. They are not laboratory values and not a basis
for medical decisions, dosing of medication or strict diets.

## 4. Medical conditions and special groups

CookSnap does not give medical, dietary or nutrition advice and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
Diet labels such as "diabetic-friendly" or "keto" describe a style of eating, not a treatment. If you have a medical
condition (for example diabetes, kidney disease, an eating disorder), are pregnant or breastfeeding, take medication that
interacts with food, or cook for an infant, a young child or an older person with special needs, ask a qualified professional
before following any recipe.

## 5. Food safety

Cook, chill, reheat and store food according to normal safe practice. The app does not know how old your food is or whether it
is safe to eat; use your own judgement, use-by dates and your senses. **Never use the app to decide whether a wild mushroom,
plant or foraged food is edible.** Following a recipe involves heat, sharp tools and appliances; take normal care and keep
children and pets away from hazards.

## 6. Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you cook, serve and eat, and for checking that it suits you and the people you cook for.
Nothing here limits rights you have by law as a consumer (see section 11 of the Terms of Service).

## 7. Tell us about a bad result

Open a recipe and tap "Report a problem with this recipe", or use Profile > Help & Feedback. We use these reports to improve
the app. If someone has had a reaction after eating a dish, contact a doctor or emergency services first.
